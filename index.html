Where this came from. Harvested from Yahoo: every season's league page from 2008 (league ID 270995) to 2025 (300772). Last updated . As a check, each team's regular-season record, points for and points against were recomputed from the individual games. All team-seasons match Yahoo's standings exactly.

Counting rules. Head-to-head records, streaks and single-game records include regular-season and playoff games (semifinals, finals and third-place games). All-time W–L and win % are regular season only, the same basis as Yahoo's standings; playoff W–L is shown separately. Consolation games are listed in the season archive but don't count. The 2026 season counts in records, head-to-head and single-game marks through its last completed week; titles, finishes, last place and season records use completed seasons only. "Era index" compares a score with that season's average weekly score (100 = average). Use it to compare a 2010 score with a 2024 one, because the 2-QB lineup and the 17-week schedule pushed scores up.

Managers before 2014. Yahoo hides manager names for 2008–2013. Those seasons were matched to people through Yahoo's per-account history and then confirmed from the league's memory. Three 2010–12 teams ran on Yahoo accounts that have since been deleted. WHAT'S IN A NAME? (2012) is listed as Yehuda S. from Avi's recollection, marked † until confirmed. The owners of fishsticks (2010) and Invincible (2011), shown in italics, aren't yet identified. If you know, tell Moti.

Waiver wire. Every waiver claim and free-agent add Yahoo kept, with FAAB prices since 2014. Season points come from Yahoo's season totals for the top 150 offensive scorers, so kickers and defenses aren't ranked as finds.

The raw archive (CSV files for seasons, teams, every game, every draft pick, every pickup and every trade) is kept in the League Records folder.
